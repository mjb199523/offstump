OFFSTUMP — A Sports Turf Facility in Latakata, Guwahati

OFFSTUMP is a dedicated sports turf and practice facility in Guwahati, built around cricket as its primary sport. Located at House No. 133, Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road, the facility offers an indoor cricket turf playing surface, professional nets, and an automatic bowling machine — all within a fully enclosed indoor environment that remains available year-round.

This page covers what sports OFFSTUMP actually supports, what the turf facility offers, and who it is designed for — so you can quickly assess whether it is the right sports turf facility for your needs.

Sports Available at OFFSTUMP

OFFSTUMP's sports turf is a cricket-specific facility. The sports currently offered at OFFSTUMP are:

  • Cricket — the primary sport at OFFSTUMP. The turf, nets, and automatic bowling machine are all cricket-focused. Sessions include batting practice on the turf against the machine, net bowling, and general cricket training.
  • Yoga — OFFSTUMP also offers structured yoga sessions at the facility for those interested in fitness and wellness as part of a broader sports and wellbeing programme.

OFFSTUMP does not currently offer football, futsal, badminton, tennis, or any other sport. If you are specifically looking for a football turf or a multi-sport facility, OFFSTUMP may not be the right fit.

The Sports Turf Environment at OFFSTUMP

What distinguishes OFFSTUMP as a sports turf facility in Guwahati is the integration of its turf surface with active cricket equipment:

  • Cricket turf surface: A quality indoor playing surface designed for cricket — suitable for batting footwork, bowling run-ups, and general match-simulation practice.
  • Automatic bowling machine (up to 130 kmph): The turf is paired with a professional bowling machine, making it one of the few sports facilities in Guwahati where you can practise batting on turf against machine-delivered pace bowling. See the bowling machine details page for full specs.
  • Indoor, all-weather facility: The turf is fully enclosed, meaning it is operational regardless of Guwahati's seasonal weather. The monsoon does not close OFFSTUMP.
  • Professional cricket nets: Full cricket nets setup within the sports turf facility for realistic batting and bowling practice.

Who Uses OFFSTUMP's Sports Turf?

  • Cricket players — all levels from beginners to competitive club cricketers
  • School and college sports teams preparing for tournaments
  • Amateur league cricket teams doing pre-match preparation on turf
  • Individual players wanting solo turf practice without a full team
  • People looking for a sports and wellness space for cricket and yoga sessions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OFFSTUMP a multi-sport turf facility?

No. OFFSTUMP is a cricket-specific sports turf facility. The facility currently supports cricket practice (turf, nets, bowling machine) and yoga sessions. Football, badminton, tennis and other sports are not offered.

What makes OFFSTUMP different from a standard sports ground?

OFFSTUMP is an indoor sports turf facility — fully enclosed, weather-independent, and equipped with an automatic bowling machine that most outdoor or open sports grounds do not have. This makes it purpose-built for serious cricket practice rather than casual play.

How do I book a session at OFFSTUMP's sports turf?

You can book online at the OFFSTUMP booking page, call or WhatsApp +91 8753912572, or walk in directly. Sessions cost ₹100 for 10 overs and first-time visitors get 1 free over.
