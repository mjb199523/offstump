OFFSTUMP — An Indoor Cricket Practice Ground in Guwahati

When cricketers in Guwahati search for a cricket ground, they are often looking for one of two things: either a full-size outdoor playing venue for a match, or a quality practice ground where they can train. OFFSTUMP is the latter — a dedicated indoor cricket practice ground in Latakata, Guwahati, built specifically for cricket practice.

OFFSTUMP is not a full-size outdoor cricket stadium or an open field. It is a professional indoor cricket practice ground — enclosed, maintained, and equipped with a quality turf surface, professional nets, and an automatic bowling machine. This distinction matters: OFFSTUMP is designed for practice quality, not for playing a full match on a 22-yard outdoor ground with a boundary.

The Cricket Practice Ground at OFFSTUMP

The playing environment at OFFSTUMP's cricket ground includes:

  • Indoor cricket turf surface: A maintained turf pitch suitable for batting footwork, bowling practice, and realistic match-simulation sessions. The surface is flat, consistent, and sheltered from weather — providing reliable bounce and pace across every session.
  • Professional cricket nets: The cricket nets are built to professional standards, giving both batsmen and bowlers a genuine playing corridor with proper netting on all sides. You can bowl at full pace and play full shots without restriction.
  • Automatic bowling machine: The bowling machine at OFFSTUMP's cricket ground is what separates it from most practice facilities. Delivering up to 130 kmph, the machine functions as a consistent net bowler — without the fatigue, mood variation, or no-balls that come with a human bowler. Details on the machine are available on the bowling machine page.
  • Flat, maintained pitch: Unlike many outdoor practice pitches in Guwahati which develop cracks, unevenness, and waterlogging through the monsoon season, OFFSTUMP's indoor pitch surface stays flat and consistent throughout the year.

What You Can Do at OFFSTUMP's Cricket Ground

  • Batting practice on turf: Use the automatic machine to face deliveries on the turf surface and develop timing, footwork, and shot-making against consistent pace. Explore batting practice at OFFSTUMP.
  • Net bowling practice: Use the cricket net to bowl at full pace on a quality ground surface — working on line, length, swing, and pace with an active batsman at the other end.
  • Pre-match team preparation: Book the cricket ground before a match to sharpen up. Teams regularly use OFFSTUMP in the days before competitive fixtures to get their batsmen set against pace and give bowlers a proper turf surface to run in on.
  • Individual solo sessions: Many players use OFFSTUMP's cricket ground for individual practice with the machine — especially useful when you do not have a full team or net partner available.

How OFFSTUMP Differs from an Outdoor Cricket Ground

It is worth being clear about what OFFSTUMP offers and what it does not, so you can make the right choice for your needs:

  • ✅ Indoor cricket practice ground with turf surface and nets
  • ✅ Automatic bowling machine — solo practice possible
  • ✅ Year-round, all-weather availability
  • ✅ Pay-to-play — no membership required
  • ✅ Professional-grade playing environment
  • ❌ Not a full-size outdoor cricket ground or stadium
  • ❌ Not suitable for a full match with fielders and boundary
  • ❌ Not an outdoor turf ground with natural grass

If what you need is a practice ground that provides the highest-quality batting and bowling environment in an accessible, bookable indoor setting — OFFSTUMP fits that well.
