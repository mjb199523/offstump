What is Automatic Cricket Practice?

Automatic cricket practice uses a machine to deliver balls at consistent, programmable speeds and lengths — without needing a human bowler. Instead of relying on a net bowler whose pace and accuracy can vary, you face the same delivery repeatedly, giving you the focused repetition that genuine batting improvement demands.

At OFFSTUMP, Guwahati's dedicated cricket practice arena in Latakata, our automatic bowling machine sits at the heart of every session. Whether you are a school-level player facing pace for the first time or an experienced cricketer fine-tuning your technique, the machine delivers exactly what you need.

How OFFSTUMP's Automatic Bowling Machine Works

The automatic bowling machine at OFFSTUMP is a professional-grade device designed to replicate real fast bowling with mechanical precision. Here is how it works in your session:

  • Speed range: The machine is adjustable from gentle medium pace all the way up to 130 kmph — matching the pace of professional fast bowlers. Your session operator sets the speed to suit your skill level before your overs begin.
  • Delivery length control: The machine's angle and trajectory are calibrated to deliver full-length balls, good-length deliveries, and short-pitched bouncers, giving you a varied and realistic batting workout.
  • Swing simulation: The machine can introduce lateral movement into deliveries, allowing batsmen to practise playing swing — one of the most difficult skills to train without a quality bowler.
  • Consistent repetition: Unlike a human bowler who tires or loses rhythm, the machine delivers each ball with the same mechanical precision. This repeatability is the single greatest advantage of automatic cricket practice.
  • Indoor setting: All sessions take place inside OFFSTUMP's professional indoor nets, which means a flat, maintained pitch surface and complete safety at all delivery speeds.

Who is Automatic Cricket Practice For?

Automatic cricket practice at OFFSTUMP is designed for everyone — there is no minimum skill level required, and no previous experience with a bowling machine is needed.

  • Beginners and school players: The machine can be set to comfortable, slower speeds so that young or new batsmen can build their technique, timing, and confidence against pace gradually.
  • Club and league cricketers: Players preparing for weekend matches use the machine to sharpen their match-readiness — especially useful for getting used to pace after a gap between games.
  • Advanced and competitive batsmen: At 120–130 kmph, the machine challenges even experienced batsmen and provides the high-intensity training that competitive cricket demands.
  • Anyone curious about cricket: OFFSTUMP offers a free 1-over trial for everyone — walk in, face the machine, and see what batting practice feels like. No booking required for the trial.
