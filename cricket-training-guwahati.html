A Real Cricket Training Environment in Guwahati

When cricketers in Guwahati search for cricket training, they are often looking for two very different things: a coaching academy where someone teaches them the game, or a training environment where they can put in dedicated practice on their own terms. OFFSTUMP is the latter.

OFFSTUMP is not a formal cricket coaching academy. It is a professional indoor cricket training facility — a place where you come with your game, and you leave having put in serious, focused work. The automatic bowling machine, the professional indoor nets, and the flat batting pitch create the conditions for genuine cricket training that most players in Guwahati have not had access to before.

Training Conditions at OFFSTUMP

The quality of your cricket training depends almost entirely on the quality of your practice environment. OFFSTUMP provides the following training conditions at its Latakata facility:

  • Automatic bowling machine up to 130 kmph: Face consistently delivered balls at any pace from gentle medium to express. The machine does not tire, does not lose rhythm, and delivers exactly what you ask for, session after session. This is the most reliable training tool available for batsmen at any level.
  • Professional indoor cricket nets: The nets at OFFSTUMP are built to professional standards. Cricket nets in Guwahati of this quality allow bowlers to bowl full tilt and batsmen to play genuine shots without restriction.
  • Flat, maintained indoor pitch: Outdoor practice pitches in Guwahati are often uneven, cracked, or waterlogged. OFFSTUMP's indoor pitch surface is flat and consistently maintained, giving you reliable bounce to train against every session.
  • Controlled indoor environment: No wind, no glare, no weather disruption. Year-round training availability is one of OFFSTUMP's core advantages in a city where monsoon season makes outdoor training difficult for months.
  • Pay-to-play flexibility: No long-term contracts or academy fees. You train when you need to and pay only for the sessions you use.

What Kind of Cricket Training Can You Do at OFFSTUMP?

Because OFFSTUMP is a training environment rather than a structured coaching programme, the kind of training you do is entirely in your hands. Here is what players typically work on at the facility:

  • Batting against pace: The most popular training use of OFFSTUMP. Face deliveries at your chosen speed — from slow practice to high-intensity 130 kmph training — and develop the timing, footwork, and composure that pace batting demands. See our batting practice page for more detail.
  • Net bowling: Bowlers use OFFSTUMP's professional cricket nets to bowl in a real batting corridor — working on length, line, swing, and pace against an active batsman.
  • Pre-match preparation: Teams and individual players use OFFSTUMP in the days before a competitive match to sharpen timing, get comfortable with pace, and work on specific match scenarios.
  • Off-season fitness training: During the months when outdoor cricket is impractical, OFFSTUMP provides a space to maintain match sharpness year-round.

Who Uses OFFSTUMP for Cricket Training?

OFFSTUMP serves a wide community of cricketers across Guwahati:

  • School and college players building their game
  • Amateur league cricketers preparing for weekend fixtures
  • Club cricket teams organising group net sessions
  • Working professionals maintaining their cricket fitness
  • Aspiring players who want consistent, quality training without enrolling in an academy

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OFFSTUMP a cricket coaching academy?

No. OFFSTUMP is a cricket training and practice facility, not a formal coaching academy. The facility provides professional indoor nets, an automatic bowling machine, and the training environment for you to practise on your own terms. Structured coaching is not offered as part of regular sessions.

What cricket training facilities does OFFSTUMP have?

OFFSTUMP has a professional indoor cricket facility with an automatic bowling machine capable of reaching 130 kmph, professional-grade cricket nets, and a flat, maintained indoor pitch surface. The facility is fully enclosed and available year-round.

Can I use OFFSTUMP for pre-match training?

Yes. Many players and teams use OFFSTUMP in the days before a match to sharpen timing against the bowling machine, organise net sessions, and prepare in a professional training environment. Sessions can be booked in advance or as walk-ins.

How much does cricket training at OFFSTUMP cost?

A 10-overs automatic bowling machine session costs ₹100. There are no membership fees or long-term commitments. First-time visitors are welcome to a free 1-over trial on the machine.
