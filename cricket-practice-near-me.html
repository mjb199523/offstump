What You Get at OFFSTUMP

When you are looking for cricket practice near you, what you actually want is a facility that is accessible, affordable, open when you need it, and equipped to give you a proper cricket workout. OFFSTUMP delivers all of that:

  • Indoor cricket nets: Professional-grade cricket nets in a fully enclosed facility. Batsmen play shots, bowlers bowl full tilt — in a safe, all-weather environment.
  • Automatic bowling machine: No bowling partner needed. The machine delivers at adjustable speeds up to 130 kmph. Perfect for batting practice on your own or in a group.
  • Flat, maintained pitch: Consistent indoor surface — no potholes, no cracks, no puddles. The same reliable bounce every time.
  • Pay-to-play — no membership: A full 10-overs session costs ₹100. Walk in and pay when you arrive. No contracts, no sign-up forms.
  • Year-round availability: Rain, humidity, or heat — none of it closes OFFSTUMP. The indoor facility is ready whenever you are.
