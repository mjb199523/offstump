OFFSTUMP's Automatic Cricket Bowling Machine

OFFSTUMP is home to one of the only professional-grade automatic cricket bowling machines available for public use in Guwahati. Located inside the facility's indoor cricket nets at Latakata, the machine is available for individual and group sessions throughout the week on a pay-to-play basis.

Unlike a human bowler, the bowling machine at OFFSTUMP delivers every ball with mechanical precision — same speed, same line, same length — as many times as you need. This makes it the most effective batting practice tool available, and the reason it has become the core feature of the OFFSTUMP facility.

Machine Specifications and Capabilities

Here is what OFFSTUMP's cricket bowling machine is capable of:

  • Speed range: The machine is adjustable from gentle medium pace all the way up to 130 kmph, replicating the speed of professional fast bowlers. The speed is set by the session operator before your overs begin, and can be adjusted between sessions.
  • Delivery length: The machine can be calibrated to bowl full-length deliveries, good-length balls, and short-pitched bouncers. The pitch point adjusts to simulate different bowling scenarios that batsmen face in actual matches.
  • Swing control: The machine can introduce lateral movement — both inswing and outswing — giving batsmen the opportunity to face and practise playing swing bowling, which is difficult to replicate with a casual net bowler.
  • Angle variation: The machine's delivery angle can be adjusted to simulate deliveries from different positions — right-arm over, right-arm around the wicket — giving batsmen practice from different angles of attack.
  • Consistent repetition: This is the machine's greatest strength. Whether you want to face 60 consecutive balls at the same length to train a specific defensive technique, or 10 overs of varied pace and length, the machine delivers without fatigue or drop in accuracy.

Why a Cricket Bowling Machine Makes Practice More Effective

A cricket bowling machine does something that even the best human net bowler cannot: it removes all the variables that distract from skill development. When you know the ball is coming at a consistent speed and length, you can focus entirely on your batting mechanics — your stance, your pickup, your weight transfer, your shot execution.

  • Targeted repetition: Identify a weakness in your game — say, playing the swinging ball — and face 60 consecutive deliveries that target exactly that scenario. This level of targeted practice is impossible with a human bowler who naturally varies their output.
  • Gradual pace progression: Start at comfortable medium pace and increase speed in increments as your timing and confidence improve. This structured approach to pace practice is only possible with an adjustable machine.
  • Solo practice: You do not need a training partner, a bowling coach, or a team to use the machine. Solo batting sessions on the machine are effective, productive, and increasingly popular at OFFSTUMP.
  • High volume in limited time: In one 10-over session, you face approximately 60 deliveries. That is significantly more active batting time than most net sessions with a single bowler, where you spend much of the time waiting.

Who Uses the Bowling Machine at OFFSTUMP?

The machine serves a wide range of players who visit OFFSTUMP's Guwahati facility:

  • Beginners and school players learning to face pace for the first time at lower machine speeds
  • Club and college cricketers building match-readiness against pace before fixtures
  • Advanced players working on specific technical weaknesses against high-speed deliveries
  • Teams and groups using the machine as part of a structured pre-match preparation session
  • First-time visitors taking advantage of the free 1-over trial

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum speed of the bowling machine at OFFSTUMP?

OFFSTUMP's automatic cricket bowling machine can deliver balls at speeds up to 130 kmph. The speed is fully adjustable and is set to match your skill level before the session begins.

Can the bowling machine bowl swing deliveries?

Yes. OFFSTUMP's bowling machine can introduce lateral movement — both inswing and outswing — allowing batsmen to practise playing swing bowling in a controlled environment.

Do I need a partner to use the cricket bowling machine?

No. The bowling machine is designed for solo batting sessions. You do not need a bowling partner, a team, or any other player. Individual machine sessions are the most common use at OFFSTUMP.

Is there a bowling machine available for beginners?

Yes. The machine can be set to comfortable, slow speeds for beginners and younger players. OFFSTUMP also offers a free 1-over trial for all first-time visitors so you can experience the machine before booking a full session.
