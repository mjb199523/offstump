A Dedicated Cricket Practice Facility in Guwahati

Finding a quality place to practice cricket in Guwahati has not always been easy. Outdoor grounds are often unavailable due to heavy seasonal rainfall, and informal practice sessions rarely offer the focused, consistent environment serious batsmen and bowlers need. OFFSTUMP was built to solve exactly that problem.

Located in the Latakata area on Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road, OFFSTUMP is a purpose-built indoor cricket practice facility offering professional nets, an automatic bowling machine, and flexible pay-to-play sessions for every level of cricketer — from complete beginners to those preparing for competitive matches.

What OFFSTUMP Offers for Cricket Practice

OFFSTUMP is a cricket practice environment, not a coaching academy. The facility gives you the space, equipment, and conditions to practise — and the rest is up to you and your game.

  • Automatic bowling machine: Face deliveries from an automatic machine adjustable up to 130 kmph. Consistent, repeatable, and available on demand without needing a bowling partner. Perfect for batting practice of every kind. Try your first over FREE.
  • Professional indoor cricket nets: OFFSTUMP's indoor cricket nets are built to professional standards. Batsmen and bowlers can practise together in a controlled, safe, all-weather environment.
  • Pay-to-play sessions: No memberships, no long-term contracts. Book a session when you need it. A full 10-overs machine session costs just ₹100.
  • Year-round availability: Guwahati's monsoon season makes outdoor cricket practice difficult for months at a time. OFFSTUMP's indoor facility is open throughout the year, rain or shine.
  • Flat, maintained pitch surface: The indoor pitch provides a consistent bounce that outdoor, uneven surfaces simply cannot match — giving you reliable conditions to work on your technique.

Cricket Practice in Guwahati — Who is it For?

OFFSTUMP welcomes every cricketer, regardless of level or background. The facility is structured to serve a wide range of players:

  • Young and school-level players getting their first structured cricket practice experience in a safe, professional environment
  • College and amateur club cricketers who want regular, focused practice between matches
  • Working professionals who love cricket and want to stay match-fit without committing to an academy programme
  • Teams and groups looking to organise net sessions and pre-match preparation in a professional indoor setting
  • Anyone who simply loves the game and wants to know what proper cricket practice feels like

Location and How to Reach OFFSTUMP

OFFSTUMP is located at House No. 133, Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road, Guwahati, Assam – 781029. The Latakata area is well connected from most parts of the city, making OFFSTUMP one of the most accessible cricket practice destinations in Guwahati.

Players regularly visit from Beltola, Zoo Road, Ganeshguri, GS Road, Khanapara, Dispur, and Basistha. Whether you are looking for cricket nets near you or searching for the nearest cricket practice facility, OFFSTUMP is Guwahati's answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I practice cricket in Guwahati?

OFFSTUMP is a dedicated indoor cricket practice facility in Latakata, Guwahati. It offers professional cricket nets, an automatic bowling machine up to 130 kmph, and pay-to-play sessions for cricketers of all levels. Located at House No. 133, Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road, Guwahati – 781029.

How much does cricket practice cost at OFFSTUMP?

A 10-overs automatic bowling machine session costs ₹100. There are no membership fees or long-term commitments. OFFSTUMP also offers a free 1-over trial for first-time visitors.

Is OFFSTUMP a cricket coaching academy?

No. OFFSTUMP is a cricket practice facility — not a coaching academy. The facility provides the environment, nets, and automatic bowling machine for you to practise. Formal cricket coaching is not offered as part of the regular sessions.

Can I do cricket practice indoors at OFFSTUMP?

Yes. All cricket practice at OFFSTUMP takes place in a fully enclosed indoor facility with professional cricket nets. This means you can practise throughout the year, regardless of rain or weather conditions.

How do I book a cricket practice session?

You can book online via the OFFSTUMP website, call or WhatsApp +91 8753912572, or simply walk in. Sessions are available throughout the week.
