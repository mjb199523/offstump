Cricket Turf Booking at OFFSTUMP — How It Works

Booking cricket turf at OFFSTUMP is designed to be as simple as possible. There is no complicated membership system, no advance registration requirement, and no minimum commitment. You book a session when you want to play, show up, and use the facility. Here is everything you need to know before making your booking.

Available Sessions and Pricing

  • 10 Overs Cricket Session — ₹100
    A complete 10-over session on the cricket turf with the automatic bowling machine. Approximately 60 deliveries at your chosen speed. Ideal for batting practice, pre-match preparation, or general turf training.
  • 🎁 1 Over FREE Trial — First-Time Visitors Only
    If it is your first visit to OFFSTUMP, you are welcome to face one free over on the automatic bowling machine before committing to a full session. No booking required for the trial — just walk in and tell us it is your first time.
  • Group / Team Sessions
    Teams and groups can book the turf for a shared session. Contact OFFSTUMP directly via WhatsApp or call for group session availability and pricing.

How to Book Your Cricket Turf Session

Choose whichever booking method works best for you:

  • Online Booking: Use the OFFSTUMP booking form to reserve your slot. Select your activity, preferred date, and time.
  • WhatsApp / Call: Message or call +91 8753912572 to check availability and confirm your booking instantly.
  • Email: Send your booking request to offstump26@gmail.com.
  • Walk-In: Walk in directly on the day. Sessions are available on a first-come, first-served basis for walk-ins. For guaranteed access, especially during busy days, booking ahead is recommended.

What You Get in Your Turf Booking

When you book a cricket turf session at OFFSTUMP, your session includes:

  • Use of OFFSTUMP's indoor cricket turf surface for your session duration
  • Access to the automatic bowling machine (10 overs = approximately 60 deliveries)
  • Use of professional cricket nets within the turf area
  • Cricket balls for machine sessions (included in the session price)
  • Indoor, all-weather environment — sessions are never weather-cancelled

Players are encouraged to bring their own bat and personal protective equipment. Contact OFFSTUMP for equipment availability if needed.
