OFFSTUMP — Cricket Nets in Latakata, Guwahati

If you are searching for cricket nets near you in Guwahati, OFFSTUMP is the answer. Located at House No. 133, Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road, Guwahati – 781029, OFFSTUMP is a professional indoor cricket facility with nets built to high standards — accessible from every major area of the city.

Unlike makeshift outdoor nets that depend on the weather or informal club arrangements, OFFSTUMP offers a fully enclosed, professional-grade cricket net environment that you can book and use any day of the week.

What the Cricket Nets at OFFSTUMP Look Like

OFFSTUMP's cricket nets are built inside a fully enclosed indoor facility. Here is what the setup provides:

  • Professional-grade netting: The nets are constructed to professional standards, ensuring cricket balls remain safely contained at all delivery speeds, including machine deliveries up to 130 kmph.
  • Flat indoor pitch surface: The pitch inside the nets is flat and consistently maintained — giving batsmen a reliable surface and bowlers a realistic pitch to bowl on.
  • Automatic bowling machine: Unlike most net facilities, OFFSTUMP's nets include access to an automatic bowling machine. Batsmen can face machine deliveries rather than needing a bowling partner for every session.
  • Indoor all-weather access: The facility is completely enclosed, meaning your net session is never cancelled due to rain, heat, or any other weather condition.
  • Suitable for both batsmen and bowlers: The net corridor is set up for genuine batting and bowling practice — batsmen can play all their shots and bowlers can bowl at full pace.

Session Information and Pricing

Cricket net sessions at OFFSTUMP are available on a pay-to-play basis. There are no membership fees or advance registrations required for regular sessions.

  • 10 Overs Cricket Session — ₹100: A standard session on the automatic bowling machine inside the cricket nets, giving you approximately 60 deliveries.
  • 🎁 1 Over FREE Trial: Every first-time visitor is welcome to a free 1-over trial on the machine. Walk in and experience the facility before committing to a full session.
  • Walk-in sessions: Drop in without a booking on most days. For guaranteed access, booking in advance is recommended.
