Frequently Asked Questions

Is OFFSTUMP's turf open during the monsoon season?

Yes. The turf at OFFSTUMP is fully indoors, so sessions are not affected by rain, flooding or any weather conditions. The facility is open year-round.

What is the session cost for turf practice?

A 10-overs machine session on the cricket turf costs ₹100. First-time visitors receive a free 1-over trial. There are no membership fees or registration requirements.
