What Makes a Turf Worth Choosing in Guwahati?

When players in Guwahati search for the best turf for cricket practice, they are generally evaluating a handful of factors: the quality of the playing surface, the additional facilities available, the location, the pricing, and the ease of booking. This page explains what OFFSTUMP specifically offers across each of those areas, so you can make an informed decision about whether it fits what you are looking for.

OFFSTUMP is not positioned as the only option in Guwahati, but as a facility with a distinct combination of features that a specific type of cricketer — one who wants quality, consistency, and convenience — tends to find genuinely useful.

Why Players Choose OFFSTUMP

Location — Central and Accessible

OFFSTUMP sits in the Latakata area on Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road — a location that is within comfortable reach of Beltola, Basistha, Zoo Road, Ganeshguri, GS Road, Khanapara, and Dispur. For players looking for a turf near them in Guwahati, the Latakata location puts OFFSTUMP in the middle of a densely populated area with excellent road connectivity.

Turf Quality — Maintained Indoor Surface

The cricket turf at OFFSTUMP is an indoor playing surface maintained to ensure consistent playing conditions every session. Because it is enclosed indoors, the surface is not exposed to rain, sun damage, waterlogging or uneven wear from outdoor elements — factors that often degrade the quality of outdoor turf facilities, particularly during and after Guwahati's monsoon season.

The Bowling Machine — What Most Turfs Don't Have

The single biggest differentiator at OFFSTUMP is the combination of a proper cricket turf surface with an automatic bowling machine. Most turf facilities in the city are just surfaces — you bring your own bowlers. OFFSTUMP gives you the turf and the machine. Batsmen can face consistent, adjustable-speed deliveries (up to 130 kmph) without needing a bowling partner. This is what makes batting practice on turf at OFFSTUMP meaningfully different from a standard turf booking.

Playing Environment — Indoor, Year-Round

Guwahati's weather — specifically the monsoon season — is one of the most common reasons cricket practice gets disrupted. An indoor turf that is available regardless of weather gives players a significant advantage: the ability to maintain consistent practice all year. OFFSTUMP's fully enclosed facility means your session happens on the day you booked it, not the day the weather allows it.

Pricing — Pay Only for What You Use

A 10-overs machine session on the cricket turf costs ₹100 at OFFSTUMP. There is no annual membership, no monthly fee, and no minimum commitment. This pay-as-you-go model means the facility is financially accessible to students, working professionals, casual players, and serious cricketers alike — without requiring anyone to overcommit before they have even tried it.

Booking Convenience — Online, Phone or Walk-In

Booking at OFFSTUMP is simple. You can reserve a slot via the online booking page, call or WhatsApp +91 8753912572, or walk in on most days. There is no complicated registration process. For first-time visitors, OFFSTUMP offers a free 1-over trial on the machine — so you can try the facility before committing to a full session.
