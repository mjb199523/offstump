OFFSTUMP Cricket Turf — Your Game, Your Session

OFFSTUMP is Guwahati's dedicated cricket turf and practice facility, located at House No. 133, Latakata-Ganesh Nagar Road. Built for cricketers who want a proper, professional playing surface to practise and play on, OFFSTUMP's cricket turf is available on a pay-to-play basis throughout the week — no membership, no long-term commitment required.

Whether you are looking for a place to practise batting on turf, sharpen your bowling on a quality surface, or simply play cricket with friends in a controlled indoor environment, OFFSTUMP provides the facility to do it. The indoor turf area is paired with professional-grade cricket nets and an automatic bowling machine capable of delivering up to 130 kmph, making it one of the most complete cricket turf setups in Guwahati.

What the Cricket Turf at OFFSTUMP Offers

  • Professional cricket turf surface: A quality playing surface designed for cricket — consistent underfoot, suitable for batting footwork and bowling run-ups, and maintained to provide reliable bounce and pace across sessions.
  • Indoor, all-weather availability: OFFSTUMP's turf is fully enclosed indoors, which means your session is never cancelled due to rain, heat, or any weather condition. This is a significant advantage in Guwahati, where the monsoon season makes outdoor turf use unpredictable for months.
  • Automatic bowling machine integration: Unlike most standalone turf facilities, OFFSTUMP pairs its turf with a professional automatic bowling machine. Batsmen can face consistent deliveries at adjustable speeds — from gentle medium pace up to 130 kmph — directly on the turf surface. This combination of proper turf and machine-delivered practice is difficult to find elsewhere in the city.
  • Professional cricket nets: The cricket nets are set up within the turf environment, giving both batsmen and bowlers a realistic, safe playing corridor to practise in.
  • Pay-to-play sessions: No memberships or annual fees. Book a session when you need it — a 10-overs machine session on the turf costs ₹100.

Who Can Use the Cricket Turf?

OFFSTUMP's cricket turf is open to everyone — there is no minimum experience level or age requirement.

  • Beginner and school-level players who want to experience proper turf practice for the first time
  • Club and amateur cricketers preparing for weekend matches on turf surfaces
  • Teams and groups looking to book a turf session for team practice or pre-match preparation
  • Bowlers who want to practise on a quality surface that provides a realistic run-up and release
  • Batsmen working on footwork and timing on turf — especially useful for players accustomed to mats who want to develop turf-specific technique

Indoor Cricket Turf — Practice Year-Round

One of OFFSTUMP's key advantages is that the cricket turf is available indoors. Guwahati receives heavy seasonal rainfall that makes outdoor turf facilities unusable for extended periods each year. OFFSTUMP's indoor turf means players can maintain consistent practice through the monsoon season and beyond — a quality that players from Basistha, Beltola, Latakata, Ganesh Nagar, and across Guwahati regularly take advantage of.

Pricing and Booking

Sessions on OFFSTUMP's cricket turf are priced to be accessible to every player in Guwahati:

  • 10 Overs Cricket Session — ₹100: A complete 10-over session on the cricket turf with the automatic bowling machine. Approximately 60 deliveries to work with.
  • 🎁 1 Over FREE Trial: First-time visitors are welcome to face 1 free over on the machine before committing to a full session.
